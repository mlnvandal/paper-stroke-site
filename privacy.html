Paper Stroke Privacy Policy

Privacy Policy

Effective November 22, 2026

Paper Stroke is a paint-by-number app for iPhone and iPad. The app runs offline and does not collect or transmit personal data to the developer. It has no accounts, advertising, analytics, or tracking.

Paintings stored on your device

The app saves your strokes and painting progress on your device so you can resume a picture. This may include Apple Pencil input, such as pressure and tilt, used to restore strokes. To clear a painting, open it and choose Start Over. Deleting the app removes its local data from that device. Your device backup settings may affect whether saved data can be restored.

Device motion

On supported devices, the app uses motion readings to change how gel ink catches the light as you tilt the device. The readings are used on your device for this effect and are not sent to the developer. Enable Reduce Motion in your device settings to keep the light still.

Apple and this website

Apple handles App Store downloads and updates under its privacy policy. This website is hosted on GitHub Pages. The site does not add analytics or tracking cookies. GitHub logs visitor IP addresses for security as described in its privacy statement.

Questions and changes

For questions about this policy, use the Paper Stroke issue tracker. Issues are public. Please leave out personal information. Updates to this policy will be posted on this page.
